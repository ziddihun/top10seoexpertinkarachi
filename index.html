Due Diligence

How to Verify a Professional SEO Expert

Anyone can call themselves an "SEO expert." Here's how to confirm they actually are one.

1

Ask for Case Studies With Verifiable Data

Look for before-and-after organic traffic data, not just vague claims of "#1 rankings."

2

Check Independent Reviews

Search for reviews on Google, LinkedIn, and industry forums — not just testimonials on their own website.

3

Ask How They'd Approach Your Site Specifically

A real expert will ask questions about your business before proposing a strategy, rather than offering a generic package.

4

Confirm They Follow Google's Guidelines

Be wary of anyone promising guaranteed #1 rankings or relying on link farms and keyword stuffing.

5

Request a Sample Reporting Dashboard

Professional SEO experts track and report progress transparently using tools like Google Search Console and Analytics.

Hiring Guide

Tips to Choose the Best SEO Expert in Karachi

A few practical filters to apply before you sign a contract.

Before You Hire

  • Define your goals clearly: traffic, leads, sales, or brand visibility
  • Set a realistic budget and timeline expectation
  • Shortlist 3-4 candidates and request initial audits or proposals
  • Compare communication style and reporting cadence
  • Check whether their specialty matches your business type

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Guaranteed "#1 ranking in 7 days" promises
  • No willingness to explain their strategy in plain terms
  • Refusal to share past results or references
  • Pushing paid link packages instead of organic strategy
  • Vague, one-size-fits-all proposals sent without any audit
